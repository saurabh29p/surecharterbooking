**A light jet carries about 6–7 guests, a midsize about 7–8, a super-midsize about 8–9 and a heavy jet about 12–14. Helicopters take about 4–6, and group charters 50 or more. The exact aircraft in your quote decides the final number.**

## The classes at a glance

| Class | Typical guests | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| Helicopter | <span class="verify">about 4–6</span> | Airport to venue, the entrance |
| Light jet | <span class="verify">about 6–7</span> | The couple and closest family |
| Midsize jet | <span class="verify">about 7–8</span> | A family group with luggage |
| Super-midsize jet | <span class="verify">about 8–9</span> | One family side, coast to coast |
| Heavy jet | <span class="verify">about 12–14</span> | The full VIP circle |
| Group charter | <span class="verify">50 and more</span> | The whole baraat |

## Why the number can change

- **Luggage:** wedding luggage is heavy. Small jets have limited hold space, so a few seats may give way to bags, or a second luggage-only flight may make sense.
- **Range and fuel:** a longer route or a hot day can mean carrying fewer people or making a stop.
- **Helicopters in the heat:** heat and altitude reduce what a helicopter can lift; the operator confirms the final number nearer the day.

## Two jets or one big one?

For 15 to 25 guests, two jets landing minutes apart are often easier to find in the season than one large aircraft, and they let guests fly from two cities. We quote both when it makes sense.
