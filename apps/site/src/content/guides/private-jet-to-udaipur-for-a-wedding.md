**Yes, you can fly wedding guests to Udaipur by private jet. Maharana Pratap Airport at Dabok takes business jets; the flight is <span class="verify">about 1 hr 15 min</span> from Delhi and <span class="verify">about 1 hr 10 min</span> from Mumbai, and most lake venues are <span class="verify">about 25–45 minutes</span> away by road.**

## Flying times

| From      | Approximate flying time                       |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Delhi     | <span class="verify">about 1 hr 15 min</span> |
| Mumbai    | <span class="verify">about 1 hr 10 min</span> |
| Bengaluru | <span class="verify">about 2 hr</span>        |
| Kolkata   | <span class="verify">about 2 hr 30 min</span> |

The exact time depends on the aircraft, the weather and air traffic.

## Which aircraft

For the couple and closest family, a light or midsize jet (<span class="verify">about 6–8 guests</span>) is usually enough. For one full side of the family, a super-midsize or heavy jet (<span class="verify">about 8–14 guests</span>). For the whole baraat, a group charter or two jets landing together. See [aircraft classes](/aircraft/).

## On arrival

Cars meet the aircraft; we coordinate the timing with your planner so the welcome at the venue happens as planned. Lake Pichola and Fateh Sagar venues are the closest; hill venues take longer.

## Helicopters

Some Udaipur venues have space for a helicopter to land and many do not. Where it's possible, the operator files the landing request, the venue owner gives a written NOC and the district gives its nod. Read [helicopter entry at a wedding](/guides/helicopter-entry-at-a-wedding/).

## Tips

- **Book early** for November, December and February dates.
- **Plan luggage** — tell us about outfits, gifts and decor before we pick the aircraft.
- **Leave a buffer** for winter morning haze.
- **Pay only the company** — Indicgems Exim LLP's current account, against a GST invoice.
