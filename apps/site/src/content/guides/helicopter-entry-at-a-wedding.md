**Yes, a bride or groom can arrive by helicopter at many Indian wedding venues — but only at a suitable landing spot, with the operator's landing request, the venue owner's written NOC and the district administration's nod in place, and only if the weather and the authorities allow on the day.**

## The landing spot comes first

A helicopter needs open, firm ground clear of trees, wires and buildings, with a safe approach path. The operator's team checks the spot, usually on a site visit, before anything else is planned. Lawns, polo grounds and open farmland near fort and palace venues often work; city hotels rarely do.

## Who handles each permission

| Who                         | What they do                                                            |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The operator                | Checks the landing spot and files the landing request                   |
| The venue owner             | Gives a written no-objection certificate (NOC)                          |
| The district administration | Is informed and, in practice, gives its approval; police are often told |
| Vaanika                     | Coordinates all of it and tells you plainly if it can't be done         |

Permissions are **coordinated, never guaranteed**. Start <span class="verify">at least 3 to 4 weeks</span> before the wedding; in the season, earlier is better. You pay the balance for a helicopter only once the permissions are in place.

## Flower showers

A flower shower from the helicopter needs its own approval on top of the landing permissions. The operator decides the height and route; petals only, no garlands or packaging.

## What it costs

Helicopters are usually priced by the flying hour plus positioning (flying the helicopter to the start point and back) and waiting time. Your quote shows one all-in price for the service, including GST. We don't publish prices on this site because they depend on the venue, the distance from the helicopter's base and the date.

## What can still stop it

- **Weather:** wind, rain, haze or low cloud. Helicopters fly in daylight and good visibility.
- **Elections and VIP movement:** the authorities can restrict flights, sometimes at short notice.
- **No-fly and restricted areas:** near some airports, military areas and monuments.
- **The pilot's decision** on the day is final.

Your signed agreement explains the options and refunds if a flight can't happen. Always plan a road fallback for the arrival.
