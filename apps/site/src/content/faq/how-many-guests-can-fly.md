---
question: "How many guests can fly together?"
group: Booking
order: 2
tags: [weddings]
---

From about <span class="verify">4–6 guests in a helicopter</span> to <span class="verify">12–14 in a heavy jet</span>, and 50 or more on a group charter.

The exact aircraft decides the final count and the luggage. See [aircraft classes](/aircraft/) for typical numbers; we can also split a large family across two jets that land together.
