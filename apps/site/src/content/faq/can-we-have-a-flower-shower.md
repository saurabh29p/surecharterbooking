question: 'Can we have a flower shower from the helicopter?'
group: Helicopters
order: 2
tags: [helicopters]
---

Where permission allows, yes — it needs its own approval on top of the landing permissions.

The operator decides the height and the route, petals only (no garlands or packaging), and the pilot can call it off on the day. We tell you early if a flower shower isn't possible at your venue.
