group: Safety

An NSOP operator is a company that holds a Non-Scheduled Operator Permit from India's aviation regulator, the DGCA, which allows it to fly charter flights for paying passengers.

Only NSOP holders may fly you for a fee. We check the permit, the exact aircraft's papers and its insurance before we offer any flight. Read more in our guide [What is an NSOP operator](/guides/what-is-an-nsop-operator/).
