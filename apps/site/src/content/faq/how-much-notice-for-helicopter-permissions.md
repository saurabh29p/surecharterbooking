question: "How much notice do helicopter permissions need?"
group: Helicopters

Start at least <span class="verify">3 to 4 weeks</span> before the wedding; more is better in the season.

The operator's landing request, the venue owner's NOC and the district's nod all take time, and you pay the balance only once they are in place.
