An advance when you accept the quote and sign, the balance 7–10 days before the flight, and any extras after it.



The advance is usually about half, and at least what the operator asks for. For helicopters, you pay the balance only once the permissions are in place. Extras such as waiting time, catering changes or extra legs are invoiced after the flight. You get a GST invoice for every payment.
