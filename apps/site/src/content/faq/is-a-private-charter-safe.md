Yes, when the operator holds a valid DGCA permit and the aircraft's papers and insurance are in order — and we check all three before we quote.

We only work with operators that hold a valid DGCA Non-Scheduled Operator Permit, and check the aircraft's papers and insurance before we quote. Your quote names the operator and the aircraft, so you can check them too. On the day, the pilot's decision is final.
