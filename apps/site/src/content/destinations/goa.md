## Getting there by private jet

Goa has two airports: Dabolim (GOI) in the south and Manohar International (GOX) at Mopa in the north. We pick the one closer to your venue. From Mumbai or Bengaluru the flight is <span class="verify">about 1 hour</span>; from Delhi <span class="verify">about 2 hr 15 min</span>.

## From the airport to the venue

North Goa beach venues are usually closer to Mopa, south Goa resorts to Dabolim; drives run from <span class="verify">about 30 minutes to over an hour</span>. Dabolim is also a naval airfield, so charter timings there can be limited; the operator confirms slots.

## Helicopters in Goa

Beach and resort venues sometimes have space for a helicopter, but coastal and naval areas have their own rules. Where a landing is possible, the same steps apply: the operator's landing request, the venue owner's NOC and the district's nod. See [helicopter arrivals](/helicopter-arrivals/).

## Best time

<span class="verify">November to February</span>: dry, calm weather. The monsoon (June to September) is not a time for open-air wedding arrivals.

## Planning tips

- Choose the airport by the venue, not by habit; it can save an hour on the road.
- Sea breezes can affect helicopter flights; agree a weather plan in advance.
