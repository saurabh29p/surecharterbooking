## Getting there by private jet

Udaipur's airport is Maharana Pratap Airport at Dabok, about <span class="verify">22 km east of the city</span>. Private jets from Delhi take <span class="verify">about 1 hr 15 min</span> and from Mumbai <span class="verify">about 1 hr 10 min</span>; the table above has Bengaluru and Kolkata too. Your quote names the aircraft and the exact timing.

## From the airport to the venue

Most wedding venues are around Lake Pichola, Fateh Sagar and the hills beyond. The drive from the airport is <span class="verify">about 25–45 minutes</span> depending on the venue and traffic on wedding days. We time the arrival so the cars are waiting at the aircraft steps.

## Helicopters in Udaipur

Some lake and hill venues have room for a helicopter to land; many do not. Where a spot is suitable, the operator checks it and files the landing request, the venue owner gives a written NOC and the district administration gives its nod. A helicopter transfer from the airport to such a venue takes <span class="verify">about 10–20 minutes</span>. See [helicopter arrivals](/helicopter-arrivals/).

## Best time

The wedding season runs from <span class="verify">October to March</span>. Winter mornings can bring haze, so we plan arrivals with a time buffer and agree a fallback with the operator.

## Planning tips

- Lock aircraft early for peak wedding dates in November, December and February.
- Tell us about heavy luggage (outfits, gifts, decor) before we choose the aircraft.
- If guests come from several cities, we time the flights so families land close together.
