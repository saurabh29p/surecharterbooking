## Getting there by private jet

Jaipur International Airport at Sanganer is about <span class="verify">13 km from the city centre</span>. From Delhi the flight is short, <span class="verify">about 45 minutes</span>; from Mumbai <span class="verify">about 1 hr 35 min</span>. For a Delhi family a jet saves the long drive on wedding-week roads.

## From the airport to the venue

Palace hotels inside the city are <span class="verify">about 20–40 minutes</span> from the airport; fort and countryside venues on the Delhi and Ajmer roads can take an hour or more. We plan cars to meet the aircraft.

## Helicopters in Jaipur

Countryside and fort venues sometimes have open ground where a helicopter can land; city venues rarely do. As everywhere, the operator files the landing request, the venue owner gives a written NOC and the district gives its nod. A helicopter can also bridge a long road transfer from the airport. See [helicopter arrivals](/helicopter-arrivals/).

## Best time

<span class="verify">October to March</span>, with the coolest, clearest days from November to February.

## Planning tips

- Jaipur is close enough to Delhi for a same-day return; ask us about it.
- Many families combine a jet for elders with a helicopter entrance for the couple.
