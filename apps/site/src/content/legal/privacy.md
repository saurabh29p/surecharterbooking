This notice explains what personal data **Indicgems Exim LLP** ("Vaanika", "we") collects, why, who receives it, how long we keep it and how you can use your rights under the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023** and the **DPDP Rules, 2025**. We are the _data fiduciary_ for the data described here.

## The data we collect

**When you enquire (website form, WhatsApp, phone or email):**

- your name, WhatsApp or phone number and, if you give it, your email;
- whether you are a family, planner or hotel, and your firm's name;
- wedding or travel city, date, number of guests and the cities they fly from;
- anything you write in your message;
- how you reached us: the page, campaign tags (UTM) and any Google Ads click id. We store a one-way scrambled version of your network address only to stop spam; we do not keep the address itself.

**When you book:**

- names of everyone flying and the government photo ID details the operator and airport require (and, where required, a copy of the ID);
- billing name, address and GSTIN for the GST invoice;
- payment records (amount, date, bank reference). We never ask for card numbers or bank passwords.

**Planners and hotels** who sign up as partners: name, firm, city, contact details, Instagram handle and approximate weddings a year.

## Why we use it

- to reply to your enquiry and prepare quotes;
- to arrange your flight with the operator, including the passenger list (manifest) and landing permissions;
- to issue GST invoices and keep the accounts the law requires;
- to keep you informed about your booking on WhatsApp, phone or email;
- **only if you separately agree:** to send occasional news and offers. This is a separate, unticked choice; you can withdraw it at any time.

## Who receives it

Only those who need it to arrange your flight or run our business:

- the **DGCA-approved operator** named in your quote (names, IDs, contact details for the flight);
- **airport, security and local authorities** where they require passenger or landing details;
- your **wedding planner**, if they introduced you, for the flight details only;
- our **service providers**, who process data for us under contract: website and server hosting, secure file storage, email delivery, and our chartered accountant for tax filings.

We do not sell your data.

## How long we keep it

- Enquiries that don't become bookings: 24 months after the last contact.
- Copies of guest IDs: deleted automatically 30 days after the wedding.
- Booking, invoice and payment records: as long as tax and company law require (usually 8 years).
- Consent records: as long as we use the data they cover, plus the period the law requires.

## Your rights

You may ask us to:

- tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
- correct or complete it;
- erase it, unless the law requires us to keep it (for example invoices);
- name a person to exercise your rights if you die or cannot do so yourself.

You can **withdraw your consent** at any time, as easily as you gave it: contact us (see **Contact** at the end of this page). Withdrawing doesn't affect what we did before, but we may be unable to continue a booking that needs the data.

We answer rights requests within 90 days, and usually much sooner.

## Complaints

Contact our grievance officer first (see the [grievance officer page](/legal/grievance/)). If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the **Data Protection Board of India**.

## Security

Data is stored on encrypted servers; guest ID copies and bank details are encrypted separately; every opening of an ID file is logged; staff access is limited to what each job needs.

## Children

We don't knowingly collect data from children except as passengers on a booking made by a parent or guardian, who gives consent for them.

## Changes

We will post any change on this page with a new date, and tell you directly if a change affects how we use data you have already given us.
