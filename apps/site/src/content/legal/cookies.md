**This website sets no advertising or tracking cookies.** If we measure visits, we use cookieless analytics that don't identify you.

## What we store in your browser

| Name               | Where           | Why                                                                                                                                | How long                |
| ------------------ | --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| `vaanika:campaign` | session storage | Remembers the campaign tags (UTM) and any Google Ads click id from the page you landed on, so an enquiry tells us how you found us | Until you close the tab |

Nothing else is stored by us. The page you see and any form you fill in are not tracked across other sites.

## Analytics

We don't run analytics on this website at the moment. If we switch on Vercel Web Analytics, it counts page views without cookies and without storing personal data, and we will update this page.

## Google Ads

If we run Google Ads, a Google conversion tag may load and set Google's cookies to measure whether an ad led to an enquiry. We will list those cookies here, and ask for your consent where required, before any such tag runs.

## Embedded content

WhatsApp and phone links open apps on your device; they set nothing on this website.
