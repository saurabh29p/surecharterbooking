**Our cancellation terms are back-to-back with the operator's:** when you cancel, the operator keeps what its terms allow, and we pass that on without adding to it. Your quote shows the exact slabs for your aircraft before you sign.

## If you cancel

Typical operator slabs look like this; **your quote is what counts**:

| When you cancel | What is kept |
|---|---|
| More than [7] days before departure | [10]% of the charter price |
| [7] to [3] days before | [50]% |
| Less than [72] hours before, or no-show | [100]% |

[LAWYER AND OPERATORS TO CONFIRM: slabs differ by operator and aircraft.]

Also non-refundable once incurred:

- fees already paid for helicopter landing permissions or airport slots;
- positioning costs the operator has already incurred;
- bank charges for the refund transfer, if any.

## If the operator or the authorities cancel

- **Weather, air traffic or the pilot's safety decision:** the operator's terms decide whether the flight is re-timed, re-flown or refunded. Where the flight can't happen at all and the operator refunds, we refund you the same amount.
- **Helicopter permission refused or withdrawn** (for example during elections): we refund what the operator and authorities refund, minus permission fees already paid. Because you pay the helicopter balance only once permissions are in place, most of your payment is usually not yet at risk.
- **Operator substitutes or cancels for its own reasons:** you receive what the operator refunds, or a replacement flight you accept.

## Changes instead of cancelling

Moving a date or changing an aircraft is often cheaper than cancelling. Ask us first; it depends on the operator's agreement and availability.

## How refunds are paid

- By bank transfer to the account you paid from, in Indian rupees.
- Within [14] days of the operator's refund reaching us, or of the cancellation if no operator refund is involved. [LAWYER TO CONFIRM]
- With a GST credit note where the law requires one.

## How to cancel

In writing — WhatsApp or email to hello@flyvaanika.com — quoting your booking reference. The time we receive it decides the slab.

Questions or complaints: our [grievance officer](/legal/grievance/).
