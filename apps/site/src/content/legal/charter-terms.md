These terms apply when you book a flight arranged by Vaanika. Your signed quote and these terms together form the agreement. If they differ, the signed quote wins.

## 1. Our role: agent, not operator

**Vaanika arranges charters as an agent. Every flight is operated by a DGCA-approved NSOP operator named in your quote.**

Indicgems Exim LLP ("Vaanika") acts as your agent to find, check and book a licensed operator. The operator holds the Non-Scheduled Operator Permit, operates and maintains the aircraft, employs the crew and is responsible for the conduct of the flight. The operator's conditions of carriage apply to the flight, and we give you a copy on request.

## 2. Quotes

- A quote names the operator, the aircraft type and registration, the route, timings, seats and one all-in price including GST, and lists what is not included.
- A quote is valid for the time stated on it (usually [48] hours), because aircraft availability and prices change.
- A booking is confirmed only when you have accepted the quote, signed these terms and paid the advance, and we have confirmed with the operator.

## 3. Payment [CA TO CONFIRM]

- **Advance** when you accept the quote and sign: usually about half, and at least what the operator asks for.
- **Balance** 7–10 days before the flight. For helicopters, the balance only once the permissions are in place.
- **Extras** (waiting time, catering changes, extra legs) are invoiced after the flight.
- Payment by bank transfer (NEFT, RTGS or IMPS) to Indicgems Exim LLP's current account; UPI only for small amounts; no cash. A GST invoice for every payment.
- We only accept payments to Indicgems Exim LLP's current account, and we never change bank details by message. Our bank details appear only inside your official quote. If anyone asks you to pay a personal account, call us first.

## 4. Passengers

- Every passenger must carry the government photo ID given for the passenger list. The operator or airport may refuse anyone without it.
- Passenger names and ID details must reach us by the date in your quote.
- Baggage must fit the allowance in the quote. Dangerous goods are not allowed; the operator decides what may be carried.
- The crew may refuse anyone who is unwell, intoxicated or behaves in a way that endangers safety.

## 5. Safety and the pilot's authority

The pilot in command decides whether, when and how a flight operates, including diversions, delays and passenger and baggage limits. That decision is final.

## 6. Helicopter landings

Landing at a venue needs a suitable spot, the operator's landing request, the venue owner's written NOC and the district administration's approval. We coordinate these permissions; we cannot guarantee them. If permission is refused or withdrawn, the [cancellation and refund policy](/legal/cancellation-refund/) applies.

## 7. Changes and substitutions

- Changes you ask for are subject to the operator's agreement and may change the price.
- If the operator must replace the aircraft (for example for maintenance), it may substitute one of similar class that has passed the same checks. We tell you before the flight. If the substitute is materially different and you don't accept it, you may cancel and receive the refund the operator gives for its own cancellation.

## 8. Cancellations

Cancellation charges follow the operator's terms, passed on without mark-up, as set out in your quote and our [cancellation and refund policy](/legal/cancellation-refund/).

## 9. Events beyond control

Neither we nor the operator are liable for delays or cancellations caused by weather, air traffic control, government or airport orders, elections or VIP movement restrictions, strikes, natural disasters, epidemics or other events beyond reasonable control. In such cases the operator's terms decide what is refunded or re-flown.

## 10. Liability

- The operator is responsible for the carriage, under its own terms and applicable aviation law, including its insurance for passengers and baggage.
- Our responsibility is for arranging the booking with reasonable care and skill. To the extent the law allows, our total liability for any claim is limited to the service fee we earned on the booking, and we are not liable for indirect losses such as missed events. [LAWYER TO CONFIRM]
- Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot be limited by law.

We use your and your guests' data as the [privacy notice](/legal/privacy/) explains, and share passenger details only with the operator and the authorities who need them.
