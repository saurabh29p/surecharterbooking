Questions families ask

Is a private charter safe?+

We only work with operators that hold a valid DGCA Non-Scheduled Operator Permit, and check the aircraft’s papers and insurance before we quote.

Is Vaanika an airline or operator?+

No. We arrange your charter as an agent. The flight is operated by the licensed operator named in your quote.

Can a helicopter land at our venue?+

Only at a suitable spot with the right permissions. The operator files the landing request and checks the site; the venue owner gives a written NOC; the district is informed and, where it asks, gives its approval. We coordinate all of it and tell you honestly if it can’t be done — for example during election restrictions.

How do we pay?+

By bank transfer to the current account of Indicgems Exim LLP, against a GST invoice. Never to a personal account.

What if the weather is bad?+

The pilot decides, and safety comes first. Your signed agreement explains the options and refunds in plain words.
